# Cold Outreach Templates — AgentVault

Three platform-specific message templates to pitch AgentVault to early users.  
Each is written to feel human, specific, and low-pressure.

---

## Template 1 — Twitter / X Direct Message
**Target:** Active retail crypto trader

---

> Hey [Name] 👋
>
> Caught your thread on [specific post topic] — the part about [specific detail] was spot on.
>
> Quick thing — how much of your day is just monitoring charts and stitching together signals from different tools? That grind is exactly what we're trying to kill.
>
> Building AgentVault — an AI trading assistant that tracks signals, surfaces what matters, and helps you move faster without the emotional noise. Your always-on analyst that doesn't sleep or panic-sell.
>
> We're onboarding our first 50 users with lifetime Pro access. Would love your honest feedback from someone who actually knows this space.
>
> Interested? Just reply and I'll drop the invite link — 2 mins to get in. 🚀
>
> — [Your Name], AgentVault

**Notes:**
- Keep it under 300 characters if possible for the opener — DMs get skimmed
- Replace [specific post topic] with something they actually posted — never use a generic compliment
- Send max 10–15 DMs per day to avoid getting flagged by Twitter

## Template 2 — LinkedIn Message
**Target:** University blockchain club president or DeFi founder

---

> Hi [Name],
>
> Came across [their club page / their post about X] — really cool to see what [Club Name / their company] is doing in the Web3 space.
>
> I'm working on AgentVault, an AI-powered crypto trading assistant that we're currently taking to its first 50 users. Given that your community is already deep in crypto, I thought a free beta access partnership could be a great fit — your members get lifetime Pro access, and we get feedback from people who actually know what they're talking about.
>
> Would it make sense to jump on a quick 15-minute call this week? Happy to do a live demo for your community too if that's useful.
>
> Thanks for your time,  
> [Your Name]  
> AgentVault — Business Development

**Notes:**
- LinkedIn works best for club presidents, DeFi community leads, and founders
- The "partnership" framing works better than "signup" — people respond to being asked to collaborate
- Always mention something specific about their profile or club — generic messages get ignored

## Template 3 — Discord Message
**Target:** Active member in a crypto trading server

**Context:** Use this after spending 2–3 days genuinely engaging in the server first (answer questions, share insights). Then send this in DMs — not in the public channel.

---

> Hey [Name], been seeing your takes in [server name] — you clearly know your stuff, especially the [recent discussion topic] breakdown.
>
> I'm building something I think you'd find genuinely useful — AgentVault, an AI crypto trading assistant. Instead of bouncing between TradingView, CoinGecko, and 3 Telegram groups, it surfaces the signals that matter in one place. No noise, no emotion, just clean AI-driven calls.
>
> We're giving our first 50 users free lifetime Pro access in exchange for honest feedback. Not looking for hype — just real input from traders who actually know what good looks like.
>
> Want me to send you the beta invite link?

**Notes:**
- Never cold-pitch in public Discord channels — you'll get banned and it kills your credibility
- Spend at least 2–3 days being a genuine community member before DMing anyone
- Discord users hate corporate language — keep it conversational, lowercase is fine
- If they say yes, respond within the hour with the link

## General Outreach Tips

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Reference something specific they posted | Send the same message to everyone |
| Lead with a question about their pain | Lead with "I'm building a product" |
| Offer value first (beta access, free tier) | Ask them to pay or sign up cold |
| Follow up once after 3–4 days | Spam follow-ups daily |
| Keep it conversational and short | Write a 5-paragraph essay |
| DM in the platform they're most active on | Copy-paste the same message across platforms |

---

*These templates are starting points — the best messages will be ones you personalize based on what that specific person has said or done online. The research time is worth it.*
